## Slide 91

- Workflow and Validation:
- The final report, utilization certificate will be submitted by PI. The submissions will be sent to research review committee -> Registrar (Admin) -> Vice Chancellor for approval/rejection/send backs with comments as attached in the previous screens.
- Once VC approves, the research project is marked as completed.
- The Universities can find the list of projects undertaken with their status under in a particular academic year.
- Document Type:
- Project Progress Report
- Utilization Certificate
- Completion Report
- Completion Certificate
- Once the completion certificate has been uploaded and is approved by the Vice Chancellor. The Research project will  be considered as closed. UUCMS SPMU Team should provide the report formats.
